About WellPath Analyst

WellPath Analyst reconstructs and compares directional-well trajectories with the Minimum Curvature Method. Each pair of survey stations is joined by a circular arc, giving TVD, North, East, vertical section, dogleg severity and closure.

Built for wellsite geologists, directional drillers and drilling engineers who need a fast, private way to check rig-site surveys, report deviations from plan, analyse formation thickness (MT, AVT, TVT, TST) and export a report. Everything runs in the browser: no cloud and no upload of well data.

Developed by Ismail Harkat — Senior Wellsite Geologist, Sonatrach (Rhourde Nouss, Algeria) — as an open-source companion to commercial survey-management software. MIT License. Contact: geoharkat@gmail.com.

The results have not been independently verified against certified software. Cross-check them before operational decisions.
